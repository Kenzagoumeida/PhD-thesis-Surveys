### Instructions for the auction


- ☐ Follow the instructions as the auction progresses, at the same pace as other participants.

- ☐ Follow all instructions carefully; do not talk with other participants during the auction.

- ☐ Florette packaged salads will be used in the bidding; each round provides more information about each salad than the previous round.

- ☐ The salads are identical; the only distinction lies in the packaging.

- ☐ In each round you will bid to purchase each salad for a regular occasion.


### Explanation of the bid


In each round you are shown two bags of Florette packaged salad and asked to write your bid for each on the corresponding bid sheet. Bids are private and cannot be shown to other participants. The auction has 3 bidding rounds; at the end, a winning round and salad are randomly drawn. The 4 highest bidders in that round pay the fifth-highest price and take the salad home. Since you do not know which round will be selected, it is best to bid your true value in every round.


### Bid steps


- ☐ Each round shows two bags of salad, numbered 1 or 2.

- ☐ You bid the amount you are willing to pay for each of the two salads on the corresponding bid sheet.

- ☐ Once all participants have bid, bids are collected and ranked from highest to lowest for each salad.

- ☐ At the end of the 3 rounds, one round and one salad are randomly selected; winners' ID numbers and the payment price are revealed.

- ☐ The 4 highest bidders for the selected salad in the selected round pay the fifth-highest price and take the salad home.

- ☐ Participants who bid below the fifth-highest price do not buy the salad and pay nothing.

- ☐ It is in your interest to bid sincerely — your true willingness to pay.

- ☐ Bidding above your true value risks paying more than you wanted; bidding below it risks losing a salad you wanted.

- ☐ Each round is fully independent; a previous round's bid should not influence the next.
