### Welcome to our survey!


Thank you for your interest and for taking the time to participate in this survey. This research, carried out by the Centre de Recerca en Economia i Desenvolupament Agroalimentari (CREDA, Spain, www.creda.es), aims to better understand people's food habits and, in particular, their adherence to the Mediterranean diet (MD). Your response is highly valuable and will help researchers explore how household characteristics and other factors relate to healthier and more sustainable food habits across different Mediterranean countries; it is therefore important that your answers be accurate and complete. This survey will take approximately 20–25 minutes to complete. There are no right or wrong answers, and you may withdraw from the study at any time. Participation is voluntary, and all answers will be treated with strict confidentiality and used solely for research purposes. If you have any questions, please contact kenza.goumeida@upc.edu or djamel.rahmani@upc.edu. Thank you very much for your time and support.
